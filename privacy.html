Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how M/s Mathuradas Meghji ("we", "us", "our"), a retail and wholesale grocery business based in Main Bazar, Okha, Gujarat, collects, uses and protects the information you share with us through this website. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to share when you call us, message us on WhatsApp, or reach out through this website, including your name, phone number and details of your enquiry or order.

  • Details shared when you call or WhatsApp us directly
  • Bulk order or wholesale enquiry details you provide
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected automatically for website performance

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries about stock, pricing or bulk orders
  • To process and confirm wholesale or retail orders
  • To communicate with you by phone or WhatsApp regarding your order or enquiry
  • To improve our website and customer service

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

4. WhatsApp Communication

We use WhatsApp to communicate with customers about orders, pricing and enquiries. Messages you send us on WhatsApp are used only to respond to you and are not shared with third parties outside of what is needed to fulfil your order.

5. Third-Party Services

This website embeds a Google Maps view to help you locate our shop, and links to WhatsApp for direct messaging. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. Standard browser functionality may store minor technical data to improve page performance.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information shared with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or WhatsApp is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
