About Us

Retail & Wholesale, Under One Roof

M/s Mathuradas Meghji, run under proprietor Pravinchandra Narandas Gokani, is a retail and wholesale grocery business based in Main Bazar, Okha. We stock everyday staples — rice, wheat and edible oil — for household retail, and supply edible oil at wholesale as an authorised dealer for RAVI, GEETA and JP oils. Whether you're buying for your kitchen or ordering in bulk for your shop, we weigh it right and stand behind what we sell.
